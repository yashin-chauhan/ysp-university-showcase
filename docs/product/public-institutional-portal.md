# 🌐 Product: Public Institutional Portal & Discovery Experience

## 1. Overview

The Public Institutional Portal ([https://uhf.ac.in/](https://uhf.ac.in/)) provides prospective students, enrolled scholars, farmers, researchers, and citizens with access to Dr. YSP University's academic programs, extension advisories, and procurement opportunities.

---

## 2. Key Public Sections

### 2.1 Admissions & Academic Programs (`/admissions`, `/about-academics`)
- Detailed program outlines for:
  - Undergraduate Programmes (B.Sc. Hons. Horticulture, B.Sc. Hons. Forestry)
  - Postgraduate Programmes (M.Sc. Horticulture, M.Sc. Forestry)
  - Management Programmes (MBA, MBA Agribusiness)
  - Doctoral Programmes (Ph.D. in specialized disciplines)
  - Diploma Programmes & Financial Assistance Schemes

---

### 2.2 Constituent Colleges & Departments
- **College of Horticulture (Nauni, Solan)**
- **College of Forestry (Nauni, Solan)**
- **College of Horticulture & Forestry (Neri, Hamirpur)**
- **College of Horticulture & Forestry (Thunag, Mandi)**
- Dynamic navigation covering infrastructural facilities, academic activities, research publications, and faculty rosters.

---

### 2.3 Regional Research Stations (RHR&TS) & Extension Centers (KVKs)
- Comprehensive station profiles for Mashobra, Jachh, Bajaura, Sharbo, and Dhaulakuan.
- Krishi Vigyan Kendra hubs across Chamba, Rohru, Kinnaur, Kandaghat, and Tabo (Lahaul & Spiti-II).

---

### 2.4 Procurement Tenders & Job Vacancies (`/user/tenders`, `/user/jobs`)
- Real-time active tender notices, RFPs, and recruitment advertisements.
- Direct single-click PDF downloads of application forms and bidding documents.
- Automatic archiving of past-due notices.
